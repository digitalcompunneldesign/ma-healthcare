Help & frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book

How does booking work?

Pick a condition, a clinic, then a doctor. Press the search button to open the clinic's booking page or send a request form. The clinic confirms by email or phone.

Is MA Healthcare Finder free?

Yes. You pay only the clinic for your visit. We never charge patients a fee.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Use the ↻ reset button in the search bar to start a new search for another patient.

Which accessibility features are available?

Use the ♿ button at the bottom right: black & white mode, high contrast, larger text, link highlighting, reading spacing, reduced motion, and a "read this page aloud" feature.

How is my information protected?

Your request is sent only to the clinic you select. We follow HIPAA-aligned practices and never sell patient data.

I made a mistake in the form — what now?

Fields show an inline message if something is missing or malformed before you submit. After submitting, reply to the clinic's confirmation email to correct any detail.
